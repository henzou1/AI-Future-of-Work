01 / UNDERSTANDING AI

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial intelligence describes computer systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI can work with language, images, data and machines. Because of these capabilities, artificial intelligence can be used across many different professions and industries.

AI TODAY

What can AI already do?

Modern AI systems can perform a wide range of tasks involving language, information, media and machines.

01

Understand

AI can answer questions, process language and translate texts.

02

Create

AI can generate texts, images, videos and other digital content.

03

Analyze

AI can analyze information and process large amounts of data.

04

Automate

AI and connected systems can perform simple computer tasks and control machines.

02 / AI & WORK

How is AI changing the job market?

Artificial intelligence does not affect every profession in the same way. Some tasks can potentially be automated, while others can be supported by AI.

01

Automation

Repetitive and predictable tasks can potentially be performed by software, artificial intelligence or machines.

  • Data entry
  • Sorting information
  • Simple customer questions
  • Scheduling appointments
02

AI Assistance

AI can support workers without replacing their entire profession.

  • Analyzing information
  • Generating ideas
  • Writing and coding
  • Supporting research
03

New Jobs

Technological development can create new professions and areas of expertise.

  • AI specialists
  • Data analysts
  • Cybersecurity professionals
  • AI ethicists

OPPORTUNITIES & RISKS

The impact of AI is not one-sided.

01

Potential Advantages

  • Fast processing of information
  • Continuous availability
  • Automation of repetitive tasks
  • Processing of large amounts of data
  • Support for human workers
02

Potential Risks

  • AI systems can make mistakes
  • Jobs and tasks may change
  • Privacy concerns
  • Questions of fairness
  • Potential dependence on AI
