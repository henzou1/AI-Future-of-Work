HOW WE USE SOURCES

Reliable information matters.

The project uses sources to explain concepts, support statements and provide context for the possible effects of artificial intelligence on work.

01

Primary sources

Whenever possible, the project refers directly to reports, studies, laws and official publications.

02

Context matters

Research findings are presented with their limitations and should not automatically be interpreted as predictions.

03

Updated research

The source list focuses on recent publications while also including important foundational material.

READING THE EVIDENCE

Research can describe possibilities. It cannot tell us exactly what the future will look like.

Reports about AI and work often measure potential exposure, task automation or employer expectations. These measurements are useful for understanding possible changes, but they should not be treated as exact predictions of individual job outcomes.
