KEY TERMS

Vocabulary

A guide to important terms used throughout this project. Use it to understand the language of artificial intelligence, digitalization and the future of work.

GLOSSARY

The language behind the technology.

These terms help explain how AI and digitalization are changing technologies, tasks and workplaces.

01

Artificial Intelligence

Computer systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing patterns or generating content.

02

Digitalization

The use of digital technologies and data to change, improve or automate processes, services and ways of working.

03

Automation

The use of technology to perform tasks or processes with less direct human involvement.

04

Machine Learning

A method in which computer systems learn patterns from data and use them to make predictions, classifications or decisions.

05

Generative AI

AI systems that can generate new content such as text, images, audio, video or code.

06

Algorithm

A defined sequence of instructions or rules used to solve a problem or complete a task.

07

Data Literacy

The ability to understand, evaluate, interpret and use data in a meaningful way.

08

AI Fluency

The ability to understand AI tools, use them effectively and recognize their limitations and risks.

09

Prompt

An instruction or input given to an AI system to produce a response or complete a task.

10

Cybersecurity

The protection of computers, networks, systems and data against unauthorized access, attacks and other digital threats.

11

AI Bias

A systematic tendency in an AI system that can lead to unfair or distorted results, often because of biased data or design choices.

12

Data Privacy

The protection and responsible handling of personal and sensitive information.

13

AI Governance

Rules, processes and responsibilities used to guide the safe, responsible and accountable development and use of AI.

14

Cloud Computing

The delivery of computing resources such as storage, processing power and software through networked services.

15

Soft Skills

Human abilities such as communication, teamwork, creativity, empathy and problem-solving that support effective work.

16

Human-in-the-Loop

An approach in which people remain involved in reviewing, correcting or making decisions about AI-generated results.
