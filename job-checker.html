HOW IT WORKS

A search engine built for real-world input.

You do not have to type the exact professional title. The checker can recognize common alternatives, variations and small spelling mistakes.

01

Exact matches

The system first checks whether the entered profession matches a stored job title directly.

02

Aliases & synonyms

Each profession can contain alternative names such as dentist, dental professional or other common forms.

03

Typo tolerance

A fuzzy matching system compares similar words, so small spelling mistakes can still lead to the correct result.
