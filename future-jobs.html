03 / LOOKING AHEAD

Jobs of the future

Artificial intelligence is not only changing existing jobs. It is also creating new areas of expertise, new responsibilities and new ways of working.

A CHANGING LABOUR MARKET

Not every future job will be completely new.

Some roles are emerging because of new technologies. Others are existing professions that are becoming more specialized, more digital or more closely connected to AI.

01

New roles

Some professions develop directly around AI, data, automation or digital governance.

02

Evolving roles

Many existing professions are changing because AI can support research, analysis, communication and routine tasks.

03

Human expertise

Human judgement, creativity, communication and responsibility remain important alongside technology.

01 / AI & DATA

The systems behind intelligent technology.

These roles focus on building, understanding and improving AI systems and the data they use.

01 TECHNICAL

AI Specialist

AI specialists work with artificial intelligence systems and help organizations develop, implement or improve AI-based solutions.

AI systems Problem-solving
02 DATA

Machine Learning Specialist

Machine learning specialists develop and improve systems that learn patterns from data and use them for predictions, classifications or other tasks.

Machine learning Data
03 AI INTERACTION

Prompt Engineer

Prompt engineers design and test instructions that help generative AI systems produce useful, reliable and task-specific results.

AI fluency Communication
04 ANALYSIS

Data Analyst / Strategist

Data analysts turn large amounts of information into insights that can support decisions, planning and business processes.

Data literacy Strategy

02 / DIGITAL & SECURITY

Protecting systems, people and digital infrastructure.

As more work becomes digital, secure systems, responsible technology and reliable software become increasingly important.

05 SECURITY

Cybersecurity Analyst

Cybersecurity analysts help protect networks, systems and data from unauthorized access, attacks and other digital threats.

Security Risk analysis
06 DEVELOPMENT

Software Developer

Software developers build, test and maintain digital applications and increasingly work with AI-assisted development tools.

Programming Creative problem-solving
07 AI OPERATIONS

AI Automation Consultant

AI automation consultants identify tasks and workflows that can be improved through AI, software and connected digital systems.

Automation Strategy
08 SOFTWARE & FINANCE

FinTech Engineer

FinTech engineers build digital financial products and systems, combining software development with financial technology.

Software Finance

03 / HUMAN & EMERGING FIELDS

Technology creates new questions, not just new tools.

As AI becomes more powerful, expertise in ethics, design, governance, healthcare and sustainability becomes increasingly relevant.

09 ETHICS

AI Ethicist

AI ethicists examine questions around fairness, privacy, responsibility, transparency and the social impact of AI systems.

Ethics Critical thinking
10 GOVERNANCE

AI Governance Specialist

AI governance specialists help organizations create rules, responsibilities and processes for the responsible use of AI.

Policy Responsibility
11 DESIGN

UX Designer

UX designers create digital experiences that are useful, understandable and accessible for people.

Creativity Human-centered design
12 SUSTAINABILITY

Renewable Energy Engineer

Renewable energy engineers develop and improve technologies for producing and using energy from renewable sources.

Engineering Green skills

AN IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

A changing job does not automatically mean a disappearing job.

AI can affect individual tasks without replacing an entire profession. In many cases, the role of a worker may change as technology takes over certain repetitive tasks and creates new opportunities for human judgement, creativity and responsibility.
